The loop

Four tabs. One habit: see it, capture it, know it.

Lumi is built around a simple cycle — the same one whether you're mining vocab from a manga chapter or a Switch RPG's menu text.

01

Capture

Snap a photo of anything with Japanese text. On‑device OCR reads it and MeCab splits it into real, deinflected words (食べた → 食べる) — shown as tappable chips colored by part of speech. Chain straight through a whole page with "Next Photo," and every capture is location‑tagged for later.

02

Cards

Tap the words you don't know. Each becomes a flashcard with an auto‑generated reading, a JMdict definition, a Tatoeba example sentence, and text‑to‑speech — filed into a deck of your choosing, with the exact photo and sentence it was mined from.

03

Dex

Every kanji you've captured lights up on a field‑guide grid — a Pokédex for the language you're actually reading. Focus the hunt on a JLPT level, or jump straight from any kanji to the exact cards and photos where you caught it.

04

Library

Photos you haven't mined yet wait in your Inbox, bundled into sessions by time and place. Swipe through at your own pace — commit a page's words, or snooze it for later.

Under the hood

Small, deliberate details for real mining sessions.

Built for reading manga on the couch and games on the go — not for typing words into a search box.

On‑device OCR & tokenization

VisionKit reads vertical and horizontal Japanese reliably; MeCab segments and deinflects (食べた → 食べる) so cards store dictionary forms, not conjugated fragments.

Bundled dictionaries & audio

JMdict definitions and Tatoeba example sentences ship inside the app, with text‑to‑speech playback — instantly, no network round trip required.

Source‑linked cards

Every card remembers the sentence it was clipped from and the photo behind it — so "where did I even see this word" is never a question.

Custom decks

Travel words, a specific game, a show you're watching — pick or create a deck right from the capture flow, with your last choice remembered.

Sessions by time & place

Captures cluster automatically into sessions — "today," "the café," "that manga volume" — browsable later on a map of everywhere you've mined.

Privacy by default

Capture, dictionary lookup, and review all run entirely on‑device — no account, no cloud dependency. Your library stays on your phone.

No Anki dread

Review a card without ever opening the app.

Lumi is built to avoid the giant scary due‑count that makes most spaced‑repetition apps easy to abandon.

  • FSRS‑6 scheduling — the same modern algorithm behind Anki's current default scheduler, not the older SM‑2 math most flashcard apps still ship. Each deck runs its own independent due/new queue.
  • Bounded, resumable sessions — configurable daily new‑card limits and review caps, so a session never turns into an unbounded slog. Stop after two cards, no guilt.
  • Home Screen & Lock Screen widgets — a single due or new card with quick grading buttons, reviewed without even opening the app.
  • Action Button & Control Center — one press into your next card, or a straight line into the camera.

Native, on‑device, offline‑first

Built as a proper native app — not a wrapper around a web view or a cloud API.

Lumi replaced a clumsy iOS Shortcut (camera → OCR → clipboard → paste into a browser) with a real SwiftUI app. Every mining session runs entirely on‑device: no photo, sentence, or word ever needs to leave the phone to become a flashcard.
